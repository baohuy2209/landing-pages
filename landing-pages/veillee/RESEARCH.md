# Veillée — creative research

The direction is an intimate, theatrical game night: forest green, candlelight, Bodoni Moda and illustrated cards. All geometry and card illustrations are original.

- [My Little Storybook, Lusion](https://exp-my-little-storybook.lusion.co/): a coherent miniature world and storytelling through composition. Veillée carries one village through changing light, rather than unrelated full-screen scenes.
- [Aten7, Immersive Garden](https://immersive-g.com/projects/aten7/): environmental progression and atmosphere. The journey moves from night to sunrise with continuous lighting and fog.
- [WebGL Scroll Sync, Lusion](https://webgl-scroll-sync.lusion.co/): close coordination between native scrolling, HTML and 3D. Object poses interpolate across chapter boundaries in either scroll direction.
- [Werewolves, Asmodee](https://www.asmodee.fr/product/loups-garous-les-blister-eco/): the underlying social deduction loop—secret roles, night, discussion and voting. The guide labels its limited three-role setup as our introductory variant.

No reference website assets, branded game artwork or source code are reused. The village, cards, mask, moon textures and orb are generated locally. Font licenses are bundled.

## Narrative revision

- [Aten7 — Immersive Garden](https://immersive-g.com/projects/aten7/): a persistent world whose atmosphere changes with the journey.
- [My Little Storybook — Lusion](https://lusion.co/projects/my_little_story_book/): cinematic framing and original crafted objects.
- [WebGL Scroll Sync — Lusion](https://webgl-scroll-sync.lusion.co/): continuity between the document and the 3D story.

Applied here as one reversible round with lasting consequences. The village, cards, wolf, shutters, lanterns and voting tokens are original procedural models; no artwork or code was copied from these references.
